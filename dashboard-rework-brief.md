# getALPHA Dashboard — Professional Rework Brief

## Direction

**Decision Intelligence Desk**: a focused operating surface for reviewing evidence, context and process quality. The dashboard should feel closer to an editorial research terminal than a generic SaaS admin panel.

## Design principles

The page has one dominant question per viewport: what matters now? Secondary modules explain why, rather than competing for attention. Dense trading information is grouped into deliberate zones with visible hierarchy: decision, evidence, context and history.

The redesign should avoid equal-weight cards, gratuitous glow, decorative gradients, generic rounded SaaS tiles and noisy market-tape treatment. Color is semantic: signal gold for action, mint for healthy evidence, amber for attention, red only for meaningful risk or loss.

## Structure

Desktop uses a compact left rail with brand mark, primary routes and account status. The main canvas uses a wide editorial header, a decision surface with a dominant conclusion, an evidence rail, a full-width market context surface and a lower analysis split. Mobile collapses the rail into a top bar and preserves the same hierarchy in one column.

## Type

Display headings use Space Grotesk with tight tracking and sentence-case copy. Supporting labels use Geist Mono in small uppercase. Body copy stays short and explanatory. Numbers are tabular and never allowed to visually dominate the conclusion without interpretation.

## Component language

Surfaces are dark graphite with fine hairline borders and one restrained inner highlight. Radius is used as a structural grouping cue rather than on every element. Tables use ruled rows and aligned columns. Buttons are compact, high-contrast and action-specific.

## States

Every live surface needs explicit loading, stale, disconnected, empty and error states. A stale feed is not presented as live. A disconnected terminal is a recoverable state with one clear action. Empty data explains what will happen next, not just that there is no data.

## First viewport hierarchy

The first viewport contains: page context, decision conclusion, one primary action, two evidence metrics, then the selected instrument and session context. Performance history appears below the decision surface so that past outcomes do not displace current process context.

## Brand essence

getALPHA is a trading process intelligence desk for traders who want to judge how they decide, not just whether the last trade won. Personality: precise, calm, accountable.
